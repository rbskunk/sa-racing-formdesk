Method, coverage and sources

Forecast snapshot covering Turffontein Inside races 3–9 on 21 November 2026. Turffontein races 1 and 2 were already past their scheduled start at the cutoff. Scratchings and unpromoted reserves carry no forecast. Past start times refer to the original card.

Recent finishes are adjusted for field size, with extra weight on the latest runs. The model also considers runs within 100 m of today’s trip, venue evidence, available final-400 m comparisons and long breaks. It uses published card ratings where coverage permits in non-handicaps. The scores are normalized and blended with equal chances, with more weight on equal chances in races containing debutants. Fair decimal odds are one divided by win probability.

This is a fixed pre-race snapshot from an uncalibrated form model. A timestamp-matched bookmaker market and validated pace map were unavailable. Card ratings are provider-published figures. Declared weights are shown as card values and may precede rider claims. Sample records use only the past runs shown.

FormFav supplied the Turffontein card. Betfair racecards, WinningForm and TAB Gold covered the field and past form checks. Raceform supplied selected historical sectionals.
